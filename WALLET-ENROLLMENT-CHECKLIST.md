The immutable registration publishes metadata and mints a public identity to the enrolling account. [Registry guide](https://docs.celo.org/build/agents/8004) · [ERC-8021 builder guidance](https://github.com/celo-org/attribution-tags/blob/main/BUILDERS.md)
2. Simulate the final tagged calldata, estimate gas from the same sender and choose a bounded gas limit above the estimate. Prefer native CELO for gas in the current adapter. Check the pending nonce, max fee and priority fee per gas, estimated L1 data and applicable operator fees, and available native balance. Budget gas limit times max fee per gas plus a reserve for rollup fees. For illustration only, 150,000 gas at 5 gwei plus 0.00025 CELO reserve gives a 0.001 CELO budget. The actual call, URI and current fee estimates govern. A zero-value call still costs gas and a reverted or timed-out attempt can incur costs. A fee estimate is not a guarantee. If the wallet balance or fee bounds are insufficient, stop and seek separate approval before any funding. [Rollup fee estimation](https://viem.sh/op-stack/actions/estimateTotalFee)
3. Ask for explicit approval of this particular mainnet registration and send, including sender, registry, metadata URI, exact tagged input, nonce, zero native value and maximum fee budget. The operator must personally review and approve the matching MetaMask prompt. Stop for a mismatch or expired estimate. On any ambiguous send preserve the intent and reconcile before considering another action.
4. Independently check the finalized successful receipt, chain, sender, contract, nonce and exact calldata. Decode the issued suffix from the actual input. Read the registry's Registered event and check ownerOf and agent wallet for the resulting ID. Only then, under separate approval for organizer-side actions, use Loops' attribution and identity checks and record their actual outcomes. A receipt or decoded tag alone does not prove Loops credit.
5. If registration is skipped or Loops does not credit its tag, stop and inspect the organizer guidance and transaction evidence. Use a later useful Daidai transaction for attribution only after separate review and approval. Do not send a placeholder transfer or repeat registration to force a check.
