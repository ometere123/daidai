# Demonstration and submission
## Story
Daidai: Two currencies. One agreement. A genuine supplier needs an exact cNGN amount, a legitimate payer with USDt authorises a ceiling. Traditional transaction-centric workflow can leave the user uncertain after interruption. Daidai tracks the original obligation, proves conversion already happened, prepares only the outstanding payout. This is not an assertion every competitor retries blindly. Compare against actual pilot user's process, and show our invariant. No fabricated cost saving or claims of full autonomous signing.

## Hard questions
- Textile already does exact output? Yes; Daidai handles authorisation and completion/recovery of whole obligation.
- Why not atomic swap+payout? EOA-bound current integration, independent evidence and recovery; atomic execution would be separate future research.
- Agent or form? State-dependent next permitted action; human confirms each financial transaction; no claim of unattended autonomy.
- Guarantee settlement? No; no liquidity/expiry/evidence may block. Correctness only within approved controlled workflow.
- What if unknown hash? Stop, retain claim, reconcile; no duplicate or automatic replacement. Owner's external transactions not under our control.
- How much did users save? Only observed outcomes, no assumed savings. 
- Why trust code? Show actual source, tests, bounded review and evidence; no independent audit claim.

## Fallback and submission
Prepare dated actual recording, verified redacted receipt/proof, offline synthetic controls and local build. RPC outage: show unresolved and recorded proof. Textile unavailable: honest refusal, previous genuine receipt. Wallet unavailable: show recorded approved action; do not execute again. Deployment unavailable: same-revision local build. If no real settlement exists, state ineligible/incomplete rather than substituting fixtures. Submission checklist: public repo and reproducible build, working public demo, actual evidence, pitch/video, architecture diagram, track selection, entry wallet/identity/tag and current credit, customer consent, exact source/deployment provenance, final rules check.
