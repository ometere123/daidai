# PDR-001 — Daidai
Status: selected, blueprint for execution | 27 November 2026 | Baseline main d8373ab3f10de65d611ee8aa44e0ec02fc671a49

## Decision
Keep Daidai, its name, existing Loops entry, enrolled EOA, ERC-8004 identity and attribution. Target Textile Stablecoin Corridors only. This is a completion/repositioning, not a greenfield rebuild or wallet migration. Core promise: Two currencies. One agreement. Verified settlement.

A supplier needs an agreed local stablecoin amount; a payer with USDt needs bounded expenditure. The transaction sequence can be interrupted after conversion and before payout. The unit of work is the immutable obligation, not an individual transaction. Daidai's differentiator is safe, evidence-driven continuation of the outstanding operation after an interruption, including refusing to repeat an unresolved or completed operation. This is **not** a claim to invent exact-output swaps, payment intents, idempotency or financial state machines. It is a conditional correctness guarantee, not guaranteed payment, cheapest FX, fiat delivery, autonomous signing or universal duplicate prevention.

## Delivery objective and decision update (PDR-002, owner correction of 27 November 2026)
The deliverable is a **hosted application a real, authorised user can use end to end**: connect a wallet and authenticate, create an invoice, share payment instructions, sign the commercial agreement, obtain a real Textile quote, complete appropriately authorised financial transactions in their own wallet, recover an interrupted settlement, and receive independently verified evidence. The public site as it stood on 27 November was a showcase plus a synthetic demo, not that product. This decision supersedes any reading of PDR-001 that treats the local pilot, the demo or the control plane as the final deliverable.

The controlled local pilot is retained, but only as the **qualification harness**: the same journal logic run against a persistent local database and a loopback wallet bridge, used to qualify RPC capability, the live Textile exchange and the first real transactions under separate operator approval. It is an intermediate milestone for safe integration, never a second product and never serving customers. The synthetic demo stays isolated and labelled; it must not share routes, state, storage or components that carry live data. Public safety gates are not removed to make the product appear live: each route opens only when the functionality and security behind it have passed the gates listed in TESTING and the owner has approved the exact destination.

The acceptance standard is a real user completing a real financial obligation through the product, not a developer completing a transaction beside a polished demo. See ARCHITECTURE (hosted mode), PRD R13 to R18 and IMPLEMENTATION H1 to H6.

## Why this project
The alternatives were Uptime (stronger conceptual novelty but unbuilt supplier/production workflow), SplitClear (overlapping payment infrastructure plus dispute complexity), Ceiling (security tooling and eligibility uncertainty), Consequence (prior art and multi-service adoption), ProofCart (merchant/data dependencies). Daidai has a tested implementation, recorded identity and direct sponsor fit. No candidate has confirmed winning traction; this is a qualitative decision given time and engineering resources, not a probability estimate. Do not reopen ideation absent new evidence invalidating eligibility or feasibility.

## Customer and proposition
Primary payer: business/agency with existing USDt on Celo and a genuine obligation to an independent Nigerian supplier accepting cNGN. Supplier: contractor/service business that values exact local stablecoin receipt. Pilot: real invoice for existing work, approved cap, independent recipient; payer retains custody. The initial pilot payer must be the enrolled EOA owner using legitimately owned funds for an actual commercial obligation; if no such obligation exists, obtain organiser guidance on external payer attribution before widening the wallet model. Do not pool customer funds or fund wallets to manufacture use. A recipient wallet may be external even if the payer is the entrant; distinguish eligibility from independent-user scoring.

## Judging
[Official event](https://www.loops.house/agents-open-for-business) reviewed 27 Nov: Textile first $1,000; quality of execution, value, tagged initial traction, named user/completed job each 25%. Mainnet conversion on Textile and external local stablecoin payment, ERC-8004 from enrolled wallet, attribution and public buildable repo; confirm full current rules including submission deadline Dec 7 09:00 UTC (10:00 WAT) and Dec 4 review. No self-trading, arbitrage or maker activity for credit; independence is not manufactured by a different address. Registration alone cannot qualify.

## Scope lock
P0 requirements R01–R12 in [PRD](PRD.md) are authoritative. P0: one USDt/cNGN corridor, one pilot wallet, immutable agreement, bilateral authorisation, live exact-output RFQ, bounded approved conversion and payout, durable journal/restart recovery, no blind retry, real verified proof, usable public release. P1: repeat genuine settlements, customer case study, explicit term revision before financial execution, improved timeline and dated proof snapshots (at least one genuine snapshot is P0 demo fallback). P2: nonessential notifications, extra corridors and integrations only after P0/P1 and a separate decision; not required for hackathon. Excluded: Altana migration, LLM dependency, private-key custody, speculative FX, escrow/lending, bank off-ramp, marketplaces, dispute adjudication, new smart contracts, automatic unknown-outcome replacement and production multi-tenant spending.

## Acceptance and priorities
Minimum: one genuine tagged conversion and external exact payout; record verified amounts, fee bounds, recipient confirmation, current organiser credit; demonstrate recovery of a finalized conversion after process restart without repeating it. Target (not mandatory counts): 3+ legitimate obligations, 2+ independent recipients, 100% amount matches and zero controlled-flow duplicates. Record actual elapsed time and comparison with user's prior process, not hypothetical savings. Dec 4 review-ready, Dec 6 submission-ready.
